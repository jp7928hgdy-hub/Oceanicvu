What we're building

Four causes, one tide

Clean oceans

Removing oil spills and pollution from our coastlines and open water, and keeping them clean for the long run — not just the cleanup after the fact.

Ocean recovery

Green forests

Restoring and protecting forest land, because the coastline and the canopy are part of the same system — one holds water, one holds soil.

Land restoration

Clean energy

Expanding solar power and building out EV charging infrastructure, so more electric vehicles have somewhere to plug in instead of a gas pump.

Solar & EV

Sustainable future

Better rainwater collection systems so we hold onto water instead of losing it — designed to reduce waste on the front end, not just conserve after.

Rainwater systems

Built for generations to come

Every cause we take on — the ocean, the forest, the grid, the rain — points the same direction: leaving the next generation more than we were given. That's the whole premise of Oceanic Vu.

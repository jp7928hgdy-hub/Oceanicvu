Resources & Media

Where to look, what to read, what to do

Real tools and guides for the causes we care about — tracking spills as they happen, growing back what's been lost, catching the rain, and going electric.

Track oil spills, live

A static page can't run a live map on its own, so we point straight to the real trackers that already do — one from a federal ocean agency, one built on daily satellite imagery.

NOAA — Office of Response and Restoration

IncidentNews

A running, government-maintained log of oil and chemical spill incidents across U.S. waters, updated as new events happen.

 SkyTruth

Cerulean — Global Oil Slick Map

A free, publicly viewable map that uses AI and daily satellite radar imagery to detect oil slicks anywhere in the ocean, worldwide.

Tree planting initiatives worth knowing about

A few of the larger, well-established reforestation efforts operating around the world right now — each one lets individuals get involved directly.

One Tree Planted

One Tree Planted

A nonprofit that plants trees across dozens of countries, funded largely through simple per-tree donations from individuals and companies.

 Trees for the Future

Trees for the Future

Works directly with farming communities to rebuild degraded land through agroforestry, training, and long-term land planning.

 World Economic Forum

1t.org — Trillion Trees Initiative

A global coalition tracking pledges from governments, companies, and organizations toward growing and restoring a trillion trees by 2030.

Collecting rainwater at home

Practical starting points for setting up a rain barrel or full catchment system — no special property or climate required.

Family Handyman

Beginner's Guide to Rainwater Harvesting

Covers the basic setup — gutters, storage tanks, and rain barrels — plus what harvested rainwater is and isn't safe to use for.

 Texas A&M AgriLife

Rainwater Harvesting Tips for Beginners

A university extension guide on building a simple home rain barrel system, sizing it correctly, and avoiding common mistakes like mosquito buildup.

Going electric: chargers and EVs

From installing a charger at your own house to picking your first electric vehicle — current, practical guides.

Angi

A Complete Guide to EV Charging Installation Costs

Breaks down what a licensed electrician actually does during installation, what drives the price up or down, and what to ask before hiring one.

 CheapEVCharger

Home EV Charging Guide

Explains the difference between charger levels and connector types in plain terms, so you know what to actually buy.

 Recharged

First Electric Car Buying Guide

A step-by-step walkthrough for first-time EV shoppers — how to think about range, charging access, and new versus used.

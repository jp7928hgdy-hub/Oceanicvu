Catch it before it's gone

Rainwater collection, in short

A basic residential system pairs gutters with a first-flush diverter and a storage tank — here's roughly what that gets you.

600 gal

Per inch of rain, per 1,000 sq ft of roof

That's roughly how much water a modest roof sheds in a single one-inch rainfall — most of it normally lost to runoff.

30–50%

Typical cut in outdoor water use

Homes that direct collected rainwater to landscaping and gardens commonly see this range of reduction in outdoor water bills.

2–5 yrs

Rough payback for a basic barrel system

A simple rain barrel setup is inexpensive; larger cistern systems with pumps and filtration take longer to pay back but do more.
